ROOTED — OFFLINE ANDROID LISTING DRAFT
App name: Rooted
Short description: Track hydroponic and soil plants, nutrients, reservoirs, and growth.

Track your growing space on your phone, without an account.
Rooted supports aeroponics bays, DWC jars, Kratky buckets, soil pots and custom containers. Track multiple plants in shared containers, seedlings, plant spaces, growth stages, moves and repotting.

Record pH, EC, PPM and temperature. Log water additions and reservoir changes with optional final measurements and nutrient doses. Record multi-part nutrients separately, using metric or US customary volume units such as mL, liters, gallons, teaspoons and tablespoons, plus weight units.

Keep notes, photos and a dated timeline. Open directly to your plants and quickly record care. Your journal works offline and stays on your phone. Export and restore backups through Android's file picker. Local recovery copies help recover earlier saves and imports.

No sign-in, ads, analytics or cloud synchronization.
Rooted records the care you provide; it does not prescribe nutrient doses or diagnose plant health.
